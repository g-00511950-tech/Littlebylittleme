Your beginner plan

Apartment gym · after school · start gently. Rest between sets and choose a weight you can control.

Monday — Gym A

5–7 min easy treadmill warm-up

• Leg press — 2 × 10
• Lat pulldown — 2 × 10
• Chest press — 2 × 10
• Seated leg curl — 2 × 10
• Abdominal crunch machine — 1–2 × 10

Rest 60–90 seconds. Ask gym staff to show you machine setup.

Thursday — Gym B

• Seated cable row — 2 × 10
• Leg extension — 2 × 10 (light)
• Chest press — 2 × 10
• Hip abduction — 2 × 12
• Standing calf raises — 2 × 12

Finish with 5–10 minutes of comfortable walking.

Other days

• Tuesday & Friday: 20–30 minute brisk walk
• Saturday: chair squats 2×8, wall push-ups 2×8, glute bridges 2×10, bird dogs 2×8/side
• Wednesday & Sunday: recovery / rest

A busy or tiring school day can be a rest day. Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.
